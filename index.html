About the Founder

— Prue LUMORAhi, Co-Founder

"LUMORA was born with a rebellious spirit to do things differently — to do them better."

Unable to find a non-toxic toothpaste that was also sustainable, Prue set out to create her own. An ardent enthusiast of health and fitness, she has delved into various athletic pursuits — from yoga to half marathons and triathlons. My belief is that clean teeth can come with clean ingredients and a clean conscience.

Co-founder, LUMORA Naturals
